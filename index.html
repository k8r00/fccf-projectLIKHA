Who We Are

Project LIKHA is a scholarship and formation program rooted in faith and service, dedicated to nurturing the academic and spiritual growth of underprivileged yet promising youth within the FCCF community.

We believe that every child is God's handiwork — and that with the right support, they can become servant-leaders who transform their families, their church, and their communities.

"For we are God's handiwork, created in Christ Jesus to do good works, which God prepared in advance for us to do." — Ephesians 2:10
Mission

To provide holistic support—financial, spiritual, academic, and personal—to underprivileged yet academically promising youth within the church, empowering them to complete their education and grow as Christ-centered individuals who will serve their families, church, and communities.

Vision

A transformed generation of educated, faith-driven servant-leaders who break the cycle of poverty, live out Christian values, and actively contribute to the growth of the church and society.

Who We Serve

Our Beneficiaries

We serve underprivileged children and youth from economically disadvantaged families within the communities served by the FCCF Community, including Area 2 of Brgy. Bagong Silangan and students from Silang, Cavite.

📚

Academic Promise

Students who demonstrate strong academic potential and a genuine commitment to learning, regardless of the financial barriers they face.

🙏

Church Participation

Youth who are committed to personal growth and active involvement in church life, reflecting a heart for faith and community.

🏡

Verified Financial Need

Families screened through home visits and barangay certification to ensure support reaches those who need it most.

👨‍👩‍👧

Secondary Beneficiaries

Families of scholars who receive spiritual and social support, and the broader church community which gains future leaders and volunteers.

Priority is Given To:

Unemployed or Underemployed Households Orphans or Solo-Parent Families Students with Disabilities or Chronic Illness Victims of Disaster, Abuse, or Neglect Disadvantaged or Indigenous Communities
Our Programs

What We Do

Project LIKHA goes beyond financial aid — we walk alongside our scholars in every dimension of their growth.

01

Scholarship Assistance

Financial assistance for tuition and school-related expenses, awarded based on financial need, academic potential, and church involvement.

02

Spiritual Mentorship & Discipleship

Regular mentoring through assigned Life Group Leaders, Bible studies, character formation, and discipleship activities.

03

Academic & Life Skills Development

Tutoring, study support, career guidance, and workshops on communication, time management, financial literacy, and personal development.

04

Community Service & Ministry

Required participation in church-led service projects such as Sunday school teaching, youth outreach, tutoring, and community missions.

05

Monitoring & Pastoral Care

Semi-annual reviews of academic progress, spiritual growth, and community involvement, with counseling and intervention when needed.

06

Sustainable Donor Partnership

Church-based scholarship fund supported by donors, partner organizations, local businesses, fundraising events, and the Adopt-a-Scholar program.

Our Purpose

Our Goals

Every decision we make is guided by these five commitments to our scholars and to God.

1

Increase Access to Education

Enable economically disadvantaged children and youth to pursue and complete elementary, secondary, post-secondary, and tertiary education.

2

Promote Holistic Development

Nurture scholars spiritually, emotionally, and socially through mentorship, discipleship, and life skills formation.

3

Develop Christ-Centered Leaders

Equip scholars to become responsible, service-oriented leaders grounded in biblical values.

4

Strengthen Church Engagement

Encourage active participation of scholars and their families in church life and ministries.

5

Ensure Accountability & Sustainability

Maintain transparent governance, regular evaluation, and sustainable funding through partnerships and stewardship.

Ways to Help

Whether through prayer, finances, or your time — your contribution changes a scholar's story.

Adopt a Scholar

The Adopt a Scholar Program connects you directly with a student's educational journey through meaningful monthly support. Your recurring commitment provides consistent, reliable funding that Project LIKHA scholars can count on throughout their academic year.

Beyond your core monthly contribution, you can choose to give additional support for birthday celebrations, school supplies, nutritious meals, or emergency assistance. Your steady presence shows them that someone believes in their future, month after month.

Donate Now

Your donation empowers Project LIKHA scholars to pursue their dreams without the weight of financial worry. By supporting their education, you're not just helping them stay in school — you're giving them the freedom to excel, the confidence to reach their potential, and the foundation to build brighter futures for themselves and their communities.
